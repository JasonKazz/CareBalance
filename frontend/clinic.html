Visits = patients × visits per patient
Costs = fixed costs + visits × additional cost per visit
Per-visit revenue = visits × payment per visit
Per-patient revenue = patients × monthly payment
Operating surplus = revenue − costs
Margin = operating surplus ÷ revenue
Minimum revenue = costs ÷ (1 − target margin)
